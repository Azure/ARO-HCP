When HCPUpgradeNewAlert fires

An actual alert is firing, activated at or after upgrade start, and its alert type did not fire in the 24h before upgrade start.

Wait for completion; stop 24h afterward. Exclude upgrades lasting over 24h and alerts with missing activation timestamps. Each HCP, version and attempt gets its own HCPUpgradeNewAlert.
