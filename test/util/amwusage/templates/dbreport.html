Accounted for against AMW

The AMW reference total is the workspace's authoritative ActiveTimeSeries boundary measurement. Complete PromQL counts and disjoint successful-leaf lower bounds explain part of that reference. These are different measurements, not a strict mathematical bound.

Unaccounted / reconciliation gap = AMW reference minus complete counts minus partial lower bounds. It may include unqueried or unknown metrics, drops, timing mismatch, and accepted/stored versus platform-reported differences. This arithmetic gap is not the missing families' cardinality and is never assigned to a particular metric.

These bars always include all measured complete counts and partial lower bounds, unaffected by metric filters or the treemap checkbox. Teal = complete; amber = partial lower bounds; light gray = unaccounted. Percentages use the workspace AMW reference, never the sum of selected metrics.
